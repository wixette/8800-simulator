# Bibliography

Every source the research cites, by the key the notes use. Each entry says where the source is, whether it could be reached on 2026-10-20, its licence, and what it gives. "Cited in" names the notes files that use it.

**Assembly Manual page numbers.** [notes-documents.md](notes-documents.md) cites MITS-ASM-1975 by the page of the 102-page PDF at deramp.com, with the printed page where it helps. The other notes cite the manual's **printed** page numbers. The two differ by 15 to 18 pages, more towards the end (the front matter, errata and some inserted pages are unnumbered), so check which one a note uses.

## MITS documents and board artwork

**MITS-ASM-1975.** MITS, Inc. *Altair 8800 Assembly Manual*, 1975 (with parts/price lists effective 1 March 1975 and errata). https://deramp.com/downloads/altair/hardware/altair_8800_computer/Altair%20Assembly%20Manual.pdf (102 pp; open). © MITS 1975; no licence. Gives: parts lists (p.2–5), expander board foil drawing to scale (p.65), chassis, rails, cross member, transformer and PSU mounting (p.54–60), harness (p.13–24, p.66–71), slot order (p.74). A restored 300/600 dpi copy is at http://www.altairkit.com/manuals/MITS/Altair%20Assembly%20Manual%20Beta%203.pdf (44.7 MB; open), better for scaling drawings. *Cited in: documents, case-and-panel, boards-and-backplane.*

**MITS-ASM-1975-IA.** The same manual, rescanned by David Nowak (Retro Tech Restoration) from a pristine copy, 600 dpi originals plus OCR PDF. https://archive.org/details/mits-altair-8800-assembly-manual (99 pp; open). No licence stated. It does not include the memory address chart or the checkout procedure. A cleaner source for reading the drawings. *Cited in: documents.*

**MITS-THEORY-1975.** MITS, Inc. *Altair 8800 Theory of Operation Manual & Schematics*. 1975. https://deramp.com/downloads/altair/hardware/altair_8800_computer/Altair%208800%20Theory%20of%20Operation.pdf (26 pp; open). Also on archive.org (`mits-altair-8800-theory-of-operation-manual-and-schematics`, no licence; and `digibarn-documents-mits-altair-theory-of-operations-1975`, labelled CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 by the uploader). © MITS, Inc. 1975. Gives: dimensioned card outline (p.26, drawing 880-110), bus pin list, power supply ratings. *Cited in: documents.*

**MITS-SCHEM-1975.** MITS, Inc. *Altair 8800 schematics* (880-101 to 880-110). https://deramp.com/downloads/altair/hardware/altair_8800_computer/Altair%20Schematics.pdf (10 pp; open). © MITS 1975. Gives: power supply 880-109 (transformer ratings, capacitors). *Cited in: documents, boards-and-backplane.*

**MITS-OPER-1975.** MITS, Inc. *Altair 8800 Operator's Manual*. 1975. Local copy at `8800-simulator/docs/altair-8800-operator-manual.pdf`; also https://deramp.com/downloads/altair/hardware/altair_8800_computer/Altair%208800%20Operator%27s%20Manual.pdf. © MITS 1975. Gives: panel functions; no dimensions. *Cited in: documents, boards-and-backplane.*

**MITS-ART-CPU.** MITS, Inc. *8800 CPU Board X1* artwork (front and back copper, silkscreen, drilling guide, component legend). http://bitsavers.org/pdf/mits/8800/Altair_8800_CPU_Bd_X1.pdf (5 pp, 300 ppi 1-bit scan; open). No notice on the pages; MITS work, treat as © MITS 1975. Gives: 1:1 board outline, tab and part positions (scale verified on the edge fingers). *Cited in: documents.*

**MITS-ART-DC.** MITS, Inc. *8800 Display/Control Board X1* artwork. http://www.bitsavers.org/pdf/mits/8800/Altair_8800_DisplayControl_BD_X1.pdf (open; needs a browser user-agent). © MITS. Gives: 1:1 pad positions: 0.60 / 0.90 in pitch, 1.19–1.21 in row spacing; drilling guide (switch holes 1/16 in). *Cited in: documents, case-and-panel.*

**MITS-ART-1K.** MITS, Inc. *8800 1K Static Memory Board X1* artwork. http://bitsavers.org/pdf/mits/8800/Altair_8800_1K_Stat_Mem_Bd_X1.pdf (5 pp; open). As above. Gives: 1:1 outline of the 88-MCS / 1K memory card. *Cited in: documents.*

**MITS-BUSDEF.** MITS, Inc. *Altair 8800 Bus Definition*. http://bitsavers.org/pdf/mits/8800/Altair_8800_Bus_Definition.pdf (1 p; open). Gives: pin list only. *Cited in: documents.*

**MITS-4MCS-1976.** MITS, Inc. *88-4MCS 4K Static RAM Board, Rev 0* (assembly, theory, schematics, troubleshooting; parts list April 1976). https://deramp.com/downloads/altair/hardware/MITS%2088-4MCS%204K%20Static%20RAM.pdf (45 pp; open); also archive.org `mits-4-k-static-ram-board-documentation`. © MITS 1976. Gives: parts list (2 card guides, 1 connector, large heat sink), tab length limit, card orientation. *Cited in: documents.*

**MITS-SIO-1975.** MITS, Inc. *88-SIO Serial I/O Board documentation* (Rev 0 & 1). https://deramp.com/downloads/altair/hardware/sio_serial_interface/88-SIO%20Rev%200%20%26%201.pdf (58 pp; open). © MITS 1975. Gives: card orientation, back-panel openings. *Cited in: documents.*

**MITS-2SIO-1976.** MITS, Inc. *88-2SIO Serial I/O Board user's manual*. https://deramp.com/downloads/altair/hardware/2sio_serial_interface/Altair%202SIO%20User%27s%20Manual.pdf (47 pp; open); a March 1977 edition is at http://bitsavers.org/pdf/mits/8800/Altair_88-2-SIO_Documentation_197703.pdf. MITS copyright (date not legible in this scan). Gives: parts list, card orientation, 25-pin connectors on the back panel. *Cited in: documents.*

**MITS-PRICE-1975.** MITS, Inc. *Altair 8800 Price List*, 1 April 1975. http://bitsavers.org/pdf/mits/8800/Altair_PriceList_19750401.pdf (2 pp; open). Gives: 4 slots in the basic unit; 88-EC, 88-EBC, 88-FAN options. *Cited in: documents.*

**MITS-CAT-1975.** MITS, Inc. *The Age of Altair* catalogue, 1975. http://bitsavers.org/pdf/mits/8800/Altair_Age_Of_Altair_Catalog-1975.pdf (24 pp; open). Gives: no 8800 dimensions. *Cited in: documents.*

**MITS-8800B-1977.** MITS, Inc. *Altair 8800b Documentation*, May 1977. http://bitsavers.org/pdf/mits/8800/Altair_8800b_Documentation_197705.pdf (195 pp; open). Out of scope (8800b). Listed so it is not mistaken for the 8800. *Cited in: documents.*

**DERAMP-CPUVER.** Mike Douglas. *Original Altair 8800 CPU Board, Rev 0 versus Rev 1*. https://deramp.com/downloads/altair/hardware/altair_8800_computer/Altair%208800%20CPU%20Versions.pdf (1 p; open; modern note). Gives: Rev 1 adds component holes for C4/C5 and a bus pin 20 ground jumper; no layout change of note for a model. *Cited in: documents.*

**BYTE-1976-10.** *BYTE* no. 14, October 1976: MITS pages "Altair 8800a Technical Information" (pp. 23–24 per NMAH) and "Altair 8800b Technical Information". https://archive.org/details/byte-magazine-1976-10 (open; OCR text). © BYTE/MITS. Gives: 8800a changes (power supply, fan, 18-slot motherboard, flat 50 % longer switch handles); 8800b changes (new panel functions, power supply, ribbon cables, 18-slot motherboard, new multi-colour dress panel with mylar). *Cited in: case-and-panel.*

**BYTE-1975-09.** *BYTE* no. 1, September 1975: MITS Altair advert (p.~6) and Processor Technology advert for the MB-1 motherboard. https://archive.org/details/byte-magazine-1975-09 (open). © the advertisers. Gives: one 4-slot expander standard, 3 more fit, 16 boards in the case, fan provision; MB-1 is 1/8 in thick and replaces 3 or 4 88-ECs. *Cited in: boards-and-backplane.*

**MITS-ADS-1975-76.** MITS advertisements in Radio-Electronics (May and August 1975) and Byte (January and April 1976), scans on Commons. 1975–1976. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Altair_4K_Static_-_Apr_1976_Byte_advert.jpg (and siblings). Access: open. Licence: public domain (PD-US-no notice). Gives: halftone photos of the kit, 4K memory boards, I/O cards, and a top view of the 88-4MCS. *Cited in: value-check-and-photos.*

**WIKIPEDIA-ALTAIR.** Wikipedia, "Altair 8800". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altair_8800 (CC BY-SA 4.0). Gives: two-piece case; base plate with front and rear; C-shaped lid. *Cited in: case-and-panel.*

**APEM-5236.** APEM 5236AB toggle switch, distributor listings (Master Electronics, Özdisan), read via search 2026-10-20. Commercial datasheet. Gives: 1/4-40 bushing (6.35 mm) with keyway, 6.5 mm panel cut-out, 10.5 mm round actuator. *Cited in: case-and-panel.*

**S100-IEEE696.** IEEE. *IEEE Std 696-1983, Standard Specification for S-100 Bus Interface Devices*, June 1983. **HJ-S100BUS.** Herb Johnson. *S-100 and IEEE-696 Bus List*. https://www.retrotechnology.com/herbs_stuff/s100bus.html (open). Gives: MITS Altair connectors 0.140 in between rows (later 0.250 in); 0.125 in pin pitch; 3/4 in connector spacing. *Cited in: boards-and-backplane.*

**CCTALK-GUZIS-2005.** Chuck Guzis, cctalk mailing list, 12 Nov 2005. https://www.classiccmp.org/mailman3/hyperkitty/list/test-drb@ccmp.vtda.org/message/4HULN6RKJDM2Y4LNHJTHVGUJV6BU23MB (open). Gives: expander boards joined by "the MITS wire-by-wire method". *Cited in: boards-and-backplane.*
